Featured Event

N.C. State Fair

The N.C. State Fair is an annual celebration that takes place between October 15th-25th that brings every communities together with entertainment, food, animals, rides, games, and family activities. The fair has allowed everyone to experience all the different parts of North Carolina in one location. To get a more in depth information on the N.C. State Fair, click below to see what the fair has to offer with a guide!

Explore the Complete N.C. State Fair Guide

What can Visitors Experience?

  • Ride carnival rides and play fair games.
  • Listening to live entertainment and performances.
  • Explore competitions and different exhibits.
  • See livestock and agriculture exhibits.
  • Visit vendors and discover North Carolina products.
  • Trying unique fair foods and treats.

Planning Your Visit?

The N.C. State Fair takes place over 11 days, giving visitors many opportunities to choose a day that works within their schedule. By planning ahead, it would allow vistors to decide their attractions, activities, and exhibits they should visit.

Visitors should check admission information and event schedules before arriving due to specific activities having their own time or due to additional requirements.
